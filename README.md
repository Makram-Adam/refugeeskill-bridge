# RefugeeSkill Bridge

### Turning refugee skills into recognized opportunities.

**RefugeeSkill Bridge** is a digital platform designed to help refugees and displaced people make their skills, experience and abilities more visible and accessible to learning and employment opportunities.

Starting with refugee communities in Kakuma, Kenya, the project aims to bridge the gap between existing skills and professional opportunities.

## 🌍 The Problem

Many refugees have valuable skills, education and practical experience but face barriers when trying to demonstrate their abilities.

These barriers include:
- Limited access to employment networks.
- Missing or unrecognized qualifications.
- Difficulty presenting practical skills and experience.
- Limited access to digital career development resources.

## 💡 Our Solution

RefugeeSkill Bridge aims to provide an accessible digital platform where users can build professional skills profiles and explore pathways to employment and further learning.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Digital Skills Profiles:** Organize skills, experience and projects.
- **CV Support:** Help users present their experience professionally.
- **Skills Assessment:** Support the demonstration of practical abilities.
- **Digital Skills Passport:** Create a shareable summary of skills and experience.
- **Learning Pathways:** Identify opportunities for further development.
- **Opportunity Connections:** Explore potential connections with employers and training partners.

*Some features are planned for future development and are not yet fully implemented.*
